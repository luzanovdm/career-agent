# Questionnaire interaction contract

This applies to all phases: setup/profile, discovery choices, application fields/clarifications, tracking/history reconciliation, interview/referral preparation and storage preferences.

## Native forms when applicable

Before asking, inspect the host's actual supported questionnaire/elicitation tools and their current-mode limits. If a tool can collect this kind of choice or free-text clarification in the present context, use it. Do not substitute a prose questionnaire for convenience, rely on an earlier test-only instruction to ask in chat, or demand a mode switch just to expose a tool. Do not claim every host has forms.

Use the native form for career-direction choices, factual/date/ownership clarification, missing achievement details, search/report/storage preferences, role selection, scoped answer confirmation and history mapping decisions. Use small coherent batches, respecting the tool's question/option limits. Offer relevant prefilled technology, achievement or contribution formulations grounded in the documents, visibly marking unconfirmed suggestions as hypotheses. Include select/edit/complete or free-text correction through the actual supported surface; avoid forcing an inaccurate choice. Do not invent numerical achievement anchors.

For free-form interview practice, use an applicable native free-text elicitation surface when available; do not turn reasoning practice into an unrelated multiple-choice quiz. Keep one practice question at a time unless the user chooses another format.

Keep conclusions, evidence, explanations and why a decision matters in ordinary chat. Put the actual questions/options in the form, not buried in that prose. Do not repeat the same unanswered question in both places. Source documents use the host's attachment/file-picker surface; text-only questionnaire tools are not upload widgets. An invitation to attach current and historical CVs with optional supporting evidence is not a reason to pretend the form accepts files.

Reuse valid user-confirmed scoped answers and recorded dispositions. Do not reask unchanged facts, answered metric batches, confidential/prefer-not-to-disclose items or already supplied files. Reopen only for new evidence, changed scope/validity or a user-requested revision. Preserve unknown/confidential/skip choices when relevant rather than making disclosure a condition of progress.

## Honest fallback and boundaries

Use a short numbered text questionnaire only when the current host/mode actually lacks an applicable supported form, including when no available tool supports the necessary answer type. State the relevant limitation briefly if useful, offer ready-made choices plus an editable/free-text answer, and continue without a mode-switch demand. Avoid a giant questionnaire even in fallback.

Ordinary intake questions must not be routed through execution-approval, permission-reset or agreement-acceptance widgets. Use real authorization mechanisms only for their intended actions, within host rules. A questionnaire answer does not authorize sending, accepting employer agreements, creating accounts or starting research beyond the user's actual choice and scope.

## Before asking: behavioral checklist

1. Is the answer already known, valid for this scope, or explicitly unknown/confidential/declined? Reuse it instead of asking again.
2. Does this missing answer change a selected direction or next action? Skip nonessential clarification.
3. Is an applicable questionnaire/elicitation tool supported in this host and mode? If yes, use it; if no, choose the concise numbered fallback and do not invent availability.
4. Are the questions a small coherent batch with evidence-informed prefilled choices, hypothesis labels where needed and free-text correction?
5. Are explanations in chat and actual questions in the form, without duplicated prose questions or a fake upload control?
6. Record the user's actual answer/disposition and scope. Do not promote a suggestion to a fact, infer consent or repeat the same batch later without a reason.

For optional context enrichment, ask once with skip and explicit source-scope choices; do not infer cross-chat access or consent. Follow [the scope and privacy contract](context-enrichment.md).
