# Useful professional messages

Read for a first draft, a substantive rewrite, or editorial review. The aim is a useful argument and a feasible next step for this reader, not a checklist of credentials. Structure follows the person, company, selected profile, relationship and requested format. A warm DM, a formal application and a project introduction can need different evidence, detail and requests; no fixed paragraph count or career-growth story is required.

## Establish the inputs

Use the verified source bank, later user corrections, selected goal/profile, the recipient's likely decision and the user's voice. Apply the [source-lineage intake rule](profile-tracks.md) to agent handoffs and summaries. Keep original assertions, personal versus team responsibility, decisions, results, measurement context and disclosure separate. A delivered capability or qualitative result can be useful without a number. Where an episode matters, understand what the person actually did and what the team/system achieved; do not invent a motivation, lesson, causal link or scale to complete a story.

The old packet is a starting point, not the complete candidate. Inspect context.claim_frontier: it previews up to five confirmed/disclosable facts outside the linked context. For reconsidered positioning or new evidence, use context --task profile --claim-scope bank with its cursors, then exact --claim-id/--source-id. omitted_by_scope and truncated excerpts are not proof that the remaining facts are irrelevant. Reconsider the relevant track claim_ids when confirmed corrections or strengths change. Keep this intake reusable; do not dump the whole database into every letter prompt.

Record factual corrections through source.capture/claim.save. Record actual general writing preferences through answer.save (for example writing.preference, recognized track/case scope and purpose:external), with the real user statement as evidence. Approval of one text is evidence about that text, not a universal template or sending permission. Preserve selected approved examples privately if actually available/authorized; never copy a person's achievements or letters into distributed examples.

## Choose, then write

Before drafting, make a short private choice: what should this reader understand about the candidate's usefulness, which supported evidence demonstrates it, and what can the reader reasonably do next? Select and connect the evidence accordingly. Tools, years and figures can support the argument; adding them does not turn a catalogue into one. A working episode, explanation, comparison or direct answer may fit, but none is mandatory.

Confirmed current ways of working belong among the candidate's strengths. AI-assisted work is neither an obligatory paragraph nor something to discard automatically: use it when evidenced and relevant, describe observable decisions/actions and responsibility, and retain its actual limits. Do not turn agent use into independent expertise the person has not claimed. Other professions need their own supported value, not a software/AI analogy.

Write in the chosen document language and the person's appropriate voice. Keep established technical terms natural for the audience instead of mechanically translating them. Sounding human does not justify invented feelings, praise, familiarity or statements such as an unsupported lesson learned. Message selection is not destruction of the claim bank: keep meaningful secondary strengths and metrics available privately and in the appropriate resume, without squeezing every achievement into every message.

## Review the actual final content twice

Factual review compares sources, interpreted meanings and exact external wording: attribution, qualifications, numbers/units, dates, contact/disclosure and unsupported additions. Existing findings, claim_spans and coverage record it; numeric retention is mechanical evidence, not writing quality.

Editorial review reads the whole message as this recipient would: is its usefulness intelligible, does the selected evidence actually support the argument, are personal and team roles clear, do the connections earn their place, and is the next step appropriate? Check voice/terminology as well as structure. A factually accurate list can need revision; a fluent argument can still fail facts. Record concrete findings, not a generic compliment or a claimed quality score.

Use schema review-context with the actual final documents and IDs. It is read-only and returns a binding, never approval. After reading that exact content and current evidence, packet.save.review.editorial records argument, decision (approved/revise/pending), findings and binding. The normal fact review/tone_findings remain separate. A missing, pending, revised or stale editorial judgment cannot make a message prepared. Saved old factual reviews remain inspectable with editorial_status:not_recorded; do not silently renew them or historical sent versions.

The binding includes external text hashes, target/person/purpose, relevant versions and the confirmed candidate-bank token. Changed facts or new confirmed strengths require reconsideration; a private unrelated note, UI preference or general workspace revision does not. Do not merely recalculate a fingerprint on an old approval. Reuse exact selected bytes only after actual current review. Incomplete drafts remain saveable.

## Exact handoff, separate authority

Read back the selected version. Before an authorized external handoff, use schema handoff-check with the actual final external text, selected attachment references and intended recipient. It checks exact bytes/selection/readiness and excludes private materials; ready never grants permission or sends anything. The host must use precisely that checked payload. A substantive edit in the compose surface requires new review and a new check; a digest declared from an earlier draft is not inspection of the final payload. Controlled binary conversions retain byte-bound extraction checks and selected source lineage.

Keep sending authorization separate. After an actual host/provider receipt, record what happened with exact version and observed message_sha256 when available. A review/payload mismatch is retained and flagged, not rewritten as a non-send or hidden to obtain readiness. Uncertain outcomes still require reconciliation before retry. No sender or new mail system is provided here.

Enforcement covers the supported local workflow. The runtime cannot technically prevent an agent from bypassing it through an unrelated external connector; the host must follow this handoff and authorization contract.
