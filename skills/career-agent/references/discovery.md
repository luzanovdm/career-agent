# Discovery

Use [the interaction contract](interaction.md) for every intake choice, factual clarification or preference: native questionnaires are required when applicable in the current host/mode; keep conclusions in chat and avoid duplicate prose questions.
A search/find request completes with [new saved application cases](search-cases.md), including Markdown packages and verified persistence. Read that contract before researching; a chat shortlist alone is not completion.

## Start and stop

Check explicit research authorization and current pause state before each external lookup. A general request to improve a resume does not authorize research. Record the authorized scope, selected directions, hard constraints, budget/time limit and source coverage. Use [the research-tool guide](research-tools.md): native search discovers URLs; optional available local rdrr extracts public content and exposes quality/truncation checks. A URL is not readable evidence without retrieval. If a host cannot browse, ask for readable pasted/attached postings and label freshness/current status unverified; never invent openings. A pause stops new research across all workers immediately. Do not spawn workers unless the user/host permits delegation; the workflow does not require workers.

## Two complementary lanes

Search job boards and freelance/contract markets for exact opportunities. Also discover companies from product needs, technology stacks, engineering blogs, public repositories, OSS ecosystems and company career pages. Record actual evidence with a source URL, publication date when available, access date, quotation/paraphrase and confidence. Distinguish “repository uses X” from “the employer hires X engineers”. An old blog post is historical technology evidence, not proof of a current stack.

A suitable company without a verified open role is a speculative company prospect. Store it as a company with a relevance hypothesis and dated source IDs, never as an open vacancy. With a specific sourced skill/product need and valid contact route, it may become a separately typed company_outreach application case in the chosen scope. Do not inflate job totals with company observations or packet versions.

For an opportunity record: company ID, exact role/project identity, employer/marketplace role ID where available, canonical role URL, title, location/work model, contract type, salary terms as observed, requirements, source IDs and dated status observations. Preserve each original URL and discovery channel. Strip only known tracking parameters for deduplication; preserve job-ID query parameters and potentially meaningful fragments, including hash-router job paths. Mirror URLs can refer to one opportunity: verify employer requisition identity and consolidate sources. Distinct roles at one company remain distinct even with similar titles. If identity is uncertain, flag possible duplication instead of collapsing or double-counting confidently.

Relevant public employee/candidate reports, employer criticism, unofficial material and sources described as leaked can inform research. Do not exclude them merely for those labels. Preserve attribution, date, relevance and reliability; separate reported claims from verified facts, and surface contradictions. Search visibility does not prove truth or authorize bypassing access controls or reproducing unrelated sensitive data. Primary-source verification establishes vacancy status without erasing useful secondary-source context.

## Verify and assess

Use primary employer/marketplace sources to verify current status and restrictions before calling an opening current or preparing final materials. Record `open`, `closed` or `unknown` with access time and evidence. A blocked page, missing date or stale cache is unknown. No fixed timestamp guarantees freshness: recheck near preparation and before any user-directed submission. Retain historical observations and source coverage even when closed.

Build a private fit table: requirement, essential/wish-list/uncertain, supported fact IDs, transferable evidence, gap/unknown and next action. Do not reject the applicant for every adjacent framework or approximate years requirement. Do not fake experience or bypass critical eligibility, licenses, genuine language needs or explicit restrictions. Explain likely transfer using evidence and offer stretch roles honestly. Hard-constraint failures need user resolution, not concealment.

Return saved new application cases with package paths, explicit readiness, compensation evidence/uncertainty, source dates and coverage limits. Known exact roles are separately labeled revisits and do not fill the requested new quota. For each suitable new case, proceed to application-packet preparation within the search request; ask only material unresolved preferences through a native form. An explicitly research-only request may stop at saved research records. No sending, account creation or agreement acceptance occurs here.
