# Search completion: new saved application cases

Follow [evidence-preserving achievement adaptation](achievement-adaptation.md): retain original claims/metrics, interpret contribution and significance before selection, reuse grouped clarification answers, and audit the actual draft, retained secondary strengths and specifically justified exclusions before saving a resume.

A request to find suitable opportunities means new actionable application cases with saved ready-to-review packages, not a two-link chat shortlist. Reuse the chosen active workspace and all current preferences. Do not reopen an unfinished archive or search other profiles implicitly. Only an explicitly narrower research-only request ends before packages. Preparing is not permission to send, accept agreements, create accounts or reapply.

## Before research

Use a native form for genuinely missing material preferences: private compensation floor/goal or explicit no-floor/no-goal choice, currency, gross/net, period, employment type and work-hours basis where relevant; fixed same-period monthly/annual amounts do not require invented weekly hours; reuse confirmed current answers, including a newer explicit hourly answer instead of deriving one from monthly pay. Keep user-specific numbers out of reusable instructions/defaults. Persist explicit company exclusions and apply them across sources and future searches. Reuse the research scope/case types, directions, geography and language requirements; opt-in to research and pause checks still apply.

## Newness is checked before a result fills the quota

Inventory canonical opportunities/history AND current-workspace review tables, registry/index files and supplied normalized history. An empty canonical array does not make an imported exact role new. Compare employer role IDs, canonical/original/mirror URLs and evidence-backed identity aliases. Preserve the original matched record and source/date. Label an exact known role a revisit, even if it was never prepared; it cannot fill a new-findings quota. Similar titles or another role at the same company do not establish a duplicate. Ambiguous identity/unknown previous submission remains unresolved and must not become a new ready reapplication.

The optional career_search.py helper checks recognized JSON/CSV review/index/history files at Data's top level (a recognized XLSX registry requires host normalization and otherwise leaves coverage unknown) plus explicit selected relative registry_paths, storing source hashes and failures. Use host readers to normalize other supplied tables/indexes first; do not silently ignore an unreadable registry, hidden index, pagination or incomplete coverage. Failed/missing coverage is unknown, not proof of novelty. It never scans resumes, external archives or other workspaces. Employer-scoped numeric IDs need a namespace/verified alias; titles alone are not identity. A speculative company outreach case has a company/need identity distinct from advertised roles and repeat attempts.

## Income and evidence calibration

Undisclosed pay is acceptable by default, not an automatic rejection or packet blocker. Research attributable comparable company ranges and market evidence for the actual hiring geography and role/level, not merely headquarters or candidate residence. Keep source/date, currency, gross/net, period, hours/employment basis, comparable role, geography and confidence. Label employer-confirmed terms separately from company/market estimates; even a strong estimate is not an employer promise.

Only comparable employer-confirmed insufficient upper caps fail a hard floor. A low lower bound, crossing range or missing upper cap does not prove insufficient pay. Never silently convert currency, gross/net, hourly/monthly or part/full-time terms. Estimates supporting the floor keep a case with transparent pay uncertainty. With no reliable comparable evidence, say unknown without invented certainty or repetitive permission requests. Record a user's explicit exclusion of unconfirmed pay if they choose it; do not invent that restriction. Ready with pay uncertainty is ready for user review, not fully verified compensation.

Do not infer English-only teamwork, people management or a hard language blocker from the posting's language. Assess real tasks and explicit requirements, transferability and noncritical wish-list gaps. Preserve critical legal/license/language facts and unknowns honestly. Keep the floor private: no unsolicited compensation figures in covers/outreach. A required employer field uses the approved expectation/strategy and exact units, never the floor by default.

## Draft readiness boundaries

Keep user context, actual job eligibility, commercial clarifications and sending permission separate. Do not require every hard setting to appear under the identical meaning in each case. The agent normalizes supported evidence using stable meaning/dimension/covers identifiers; the user does not edit technical keys. Match only known semantic dimensions, preserve conflicting evidence and keep unrelated or unfamiliar hard settings unresolved.

- Residence is user context. A sourced geography pass under an equivalent key can cover eligibility for that residence; do not create another unresolved location check merely because its identifier differs. Actual contrary hiring/work-rights restrictions still block. Worldwide hiring does not establish that every local legal/contract arrangement is finalized.
- Classify an ordinary IE/EOR or similar commercial arrangement not yet negotiated as a negotiation clarification only when source evidence supports that classification and no contrary restriction is established. Record the unknown arrangement; do not label it agreed. Real legal eligibility, explicit employer exclusions and user-essential arrangements remain hard constraints. A mistakenly hard-tagged process detail is an input-classification issue: review and correct its classification with provenance rather than silently waiving hard input in the evaluator.
- Management-team language criteria apply to management cases, not individual-contributor roles. Use evidenced role_track/management_role or scoped applicability, not the posting language or a title guess. Unknown or conflicting management scope leaves the criterion unresolved; do not assume the role is an individual-contributor case. General essential language and stack requirements remain applicable; do not broadly waive framework proficiency.
- submission.no_sends and other recognized sending controls govern sending, not preparation. Draft readiness never grants permission to send. Pending employer privacy/legal consent is shown separately from required factual answers; a reviewable draft can retain consent pending, but cannot claim the form fully completed, consent accepted or submission authorized. Missing required factual answers still prevent a complete factual packet. Never reuse employer-specific consent automatically.
- Missing income-policy case_types is missing normalized metadata, not proof the case type is excluded. Reuse structured confirmed search scope when present; otherwise preserve the existing authorization and flag normalization without inventing restrictions, user confirmation or new research authority. An explicit out-of-scope type remains blocked. Reuse recorded salary terms when policy metadata is absent; genuinely missing/conflicting floor choices still need clarification.
- Compare same-currency, same-gross/net fixed amounts directly; normalize monthly/yearly amounts using exactly twelve months per year and retain original observations/units. This needs no weekly-hours assumption. Hourly-to-calendar, currency and gross/net comparisons remain separate; do not infer conversions. Hourly-work schedule assumptions belong to their specific scenario, not all permanent roles. No hourly/monthly conversion is introduced. Explicit essential work-hours/employment constraints are assessed separately and still apply.
- An alleged employer degree/essential requirement whose primary source was not read is unverified, not an established hard failure or a reason for categorical exclusion. Preserve the claim and verification state and check it when research is authorized. Do not pretend the user meets it. Confirmed essential failures still block; unknown actual requirements remain visible rather than being changed to passes.

The helper returns draft_ready, factual unknowns, commercial/context clarifications, pending_consents, scope normalization and submission_authorized=false separately. These are assessment results, not edits to historical source claims or an authorization mechanism. Missing optional normalized policy fields must not produce fictitious confirmations.

## Save, read back, verify, refresh

Record the requested new-case quota and batch ID; never silently lower the target to the number found. For every shown new case, save exact identity, source/check dates, private fit evidence, hard/preferred/unknown assessments, compensation assessment, status and next action. Cases may be advertised vacancies, contract projects, or explicitly labeled company outreach prospects with a specific sourced product/skill need and verified contact/application route. A prospect is not an open job; respect selected case types. No automated contact occurs.

Build the actual company → exact opportunity → distinct attempt package: private source/fit dossier, tailored resume or reference to an unchanged approved immutable resume, cover, observed application fields with safe scoped cached answers, unresolved items and explicit readiness. Write **all new resume/cover/dossier/form drafts in Markdown .md only**. General materials stay in the shared immutable library; references pin exact versions/hashes. No per-vacancy TXT/PDF/DOCX. Render PDF/DOCX only for an actual required upload or explicit request and reuse identical versions.

Inspect the final generated Markdown and actual destination fields, not just templates. Validate public links with authorized readable evidence where available; preserve unknown/broken links as unresolved. A local private resume path is never a recruiter-accessible public URL. Check saved bytes/links/hashes and required answers. Missing essential fields or prior unresolved submissions cannot be labeled ready. Employer consents remain explicit and scoped.

Read back canonical records and every pinned file, then refresh Career.xlsx or Career.csv and confirm its state hash/revision. If writing/readback/export fails, report partial completion and exact remaining work; preserve valid state and previous reports. Never fill the quota with a duplicate, missing file, unresolved required factual answer or stale view. Separate ready cases, accepted pay-uncertain ready cases, conditional packages and revisits in the final response, with actual paths. Continue within the authorized budget or report a shortfall honestly; do not silently substitute old cases.

The agent operates optional helper commands: novelty, prepare-case and report. prepare-case reserves provenance, persists records and immutable Markdown materials/attempts/artifact versions, reads back files and refreshes XLSX. Its request is schema-validated and expected-revision checked; the ordinary user does not edit JSON or run commands. Partial attempts remain visible for reconciliation. The report recomputes readiness and verifies a current XLSX/CSV; new_ready includes transparently labeled accepted pay-uncertain packages and never means an application was sent. Repeating an identical request resumes the same case, not another quota item. Helpers validate attestations/structure, not whether an agent's prose or research claims are true.

Relevance changes order, emphasis and detail; preserve secondary meaningful accomplishments and supported metrics concisely. Do not drop them through a role template, arbitrary length cutoff or blanket unselected-to-irrelevant mapping. Explain primary achievements more deeply. Mark conditional/failed drafts clearly in the private handoff; audit readiness is not semantic/editorial approval.
