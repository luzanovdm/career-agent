# Optional context enrichment

Offer this once during intake through the applicable native questionnaire, without blocking profile work. Translate the form into the user's language. Explain the benefit briefly in chat: career-relevant context may reveal strengths, solved problems, latent capabilities and private interview growth areas.

Offer: **Skip**, **Only excerpts/files I select**, or **Selected accessible chats or memory summaries**. When opting in, collect the actual named source scope and purposes in a small follow-up form, not blanket account access. Reuse a recorded skip/decision; ask again only on a user-requested change or materially changed scope. No access or research is implied by installation, another phase's consent, or approval to import application history.

Inspect actual host capabilities first. Memory summaries, session resume, selected-thread reading and full-transcript search are different capabilities. Never promise complete account history, scan hidden transcript stores or override host restrictions. Use only authorized host-exposed sources in the selected scope. Without them, accept user-selected pasted/attached excerpts or skip. Record unavailable/truncated source coverage; a URL or thread title alone is not source content. Respect research pause; opt-in does not start company/job research or authorize messaging.

Retain minimal career-relevant evidence: source locator/date/coverage, explicit statement versus inference, confidence, proposed insight and actual user confirmation. Exclude unrelated sensitive life details. Offer ready-made editable interpretations; latent capability is a hypothesis, not an achievement or qualification. Confirm career claims and their disclosure permission before translating them into externally usable facts.

Growth areas stay **private interview-preparation material only**. Never put them in resumes, cover letters, public posts, application answers, referral requests or externally shareable files. Confirmation does not remove this restriction. Raw insights never become public claims: a supported strength/solved problem first needs a separate confirmed career fact, source links and disclosure approval. Keep private growth distinct from those facts.

Persist the choice, exact scope, purposes, date and decision evidence in context_access. Store context_insights with source_ids/source_scope, kind, basis, confidence and suggested/confirmed/declined status. Use reviewed expected-revision operations set_context_access and confirm_context_insight; the agent operates helpers when available. Derived facts link context_insight_ids. Growth-derived facts require private_interview_only=true and disclosure=private. Raw insights may be referenced only by a private preparation artifact, not external documents or answer caches.

Withdrawal stops future retrieval/use in that scope. Preserve the audit trail, flag previously prepared external claims for review and exclude withdrawn context from future drafts/reuse. Do not silently re-enable historical artifacts or claim deletion of provider history. Explain retained local evidence/backups and offer a separate user-directed cleanup if requested; withdrawal alone is not destructive deletion.

Before any external artifact or form answer, check: no growth insight or growth-derived fact, no unconfirmed inference, no withdrawn/out-of-scope context, explicit disclosure permission, and no private notes accidentally included in a selected export. Complete private handoff may retain this evidence; employer-facing packaging must not.
