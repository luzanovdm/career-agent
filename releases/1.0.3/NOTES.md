# Career Agent 1.0.3

Messages should make a useful argument for the actual reader rather than list credentials. The skill now uses verified sources, later corrections, selected goals and the user's voice to choose evidence and an appropriate next step. A working episode or present method can support that choice, without a fixed chronology, paragraph count, invented motivation or mandatory AI story.

Factual and editorial review are separate. Existing immutable records bind the judgment to exact external content, recipient/purpose and material evidence; a bounded claim-bank frontier makes new confirmed strengths discoverable beyond old packet links. New content/evidence requires actual reconsideration, not rehashing an old approval. Scripts verify consistency and declared state, not literary quality or semantic truth. Old factual approvals are not silently converted into editorial approvals. Historical text and events remain intact; unsent drafts can be reassessed separately through the normal facade.

Read-only handoff checks compare the final selected payload/recipient, refuse private materials and block unresolved transmission outcomes. They grant no permission and send nothing; arbitrary external connectors remain outside the local runtime's technical enforcement. Actual reported sends remain recorded with explicit review/content mismatch evidence.

The verified bounded backup policy from 1.0.1/1.0.2 remains. Updating does not delete old unmanaged backups or reclaim their disk space; cleanup needs a separately approved exact plan. Only native macOS arm64 has installed acceptance evidence; other platforms and OS minima are untested.
